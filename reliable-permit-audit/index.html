The chat widget swallows the whole site on phones

The RingCentral chat window auto-opens on page load. On a phone-sized screen it expands to fill the entire viewport with an empty white conversation panel — covering the headline, the phone number, the services, everything. A mobile visitor sees a logo, a white void, and a chat box.

Truckers and dispatchers searching for permits are overwhelmingly on phones. Right now, every one of those visitors lands on what looks like a broken page — and Google's mobile-first crawler renders the page the same way.

Full Findings

What's broken, ranked

CRITICAL

Mobile homepage is a blank white screen

The chat widget auto-opens and covers the full phone viewport with an empty panel. Verified on repeated fresh loads. Every mobile visitor — and Google's mobile-first crawler — meets this first.

CRITICAL

Main content takes 21.8 s to appear on mobile

Google's passing grade is 2.5 s. Industry data puts the majority of mobile abandonment at ~3 s — this page is still blank at that point, and stays interactive-dead until 23.6 s.

MAJOR

3.2 MB of JavaScript, 658 KB of it never used

Page-builder scripts, sliders, and plugin bundles load on every page whether used or not. Lighthouse estimates 2.8 s of load time recoverable from unused JavaScript alone, plus 199 KB of unused CSS.

MAJOR

Slow server, no edge caching

Time-to-first-byte measured 1.7–2.3 s across repeated probes (goal: under 0.8 s), and a redirect chain adds an estimated 1.5 s more. Every page view pays this tax before a single pixel loads.

MAJOR

No business schema markup — invisible to rich results

The only structured data on the page is a navigation menu list. No Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, or FAQ schema — the things that earn map packs, star ratings, and AI-search citations for "oversize load permits" queries.

MINOR

Invalid heading structure

The homepage's main heading is an <h2> nested inside an <h1> — invalid HTML from the page builder that muddies how crawlers read the page hierarchy.

MINOR

Full YouTube player loads before anyone clicks play

1.9 MB across 20 requests for a single embedded video. A click-to-load poster image would cut page weight by 40% on its own.

The Path Forward

Keep the equity. Replace the frame.

The rebuild isn't starting from zero — it's moving 300+ articles of content equity into a site that loads in under 3 seconds and actually renders on a phone.

  1. Kill the mobile blackout — day one

    Reconfigure or replace the chat widget so it never auto-opens over content. This is the single highest-ROI fix available.

  2. Rebuild on a modern lightweight stack

    Same brand, new frame: no page-builder bloat, one bundled stylesheet, click-to-load video, edge-cached hosting. Target: LCP under 2.5 s, page weight under 1 MB, performance score 90+.

  3. Migrate all 302 articles with 1:1 redirects

    Every existing URL 301-redirects to its new home so years of Google equity transfers instead of evaporating.

  4. Layer in the missing schema

    Organization, Service, and FAQ structured data on every service page — eligibility for rich results and AI-search citations the current site can't earn.

  5. Measure it honestly

    Proper analytics with conversion events (calls, estimate requests), so "the new site works" is a number, not a feeling.
