A story, start to finish

1927. The 18th hole. Four real people.

The Carry began as a two-sentence idea from a members' club: a golfer with one shot to win, a caddie who tells him to go for it, and two announcers calling it from a wooden tower. The four leads are real members, cast from a handful of photos. There was no set, no crew and no 1927. There was a story.

Questions

Before the call

Do I have to be on camera?

No. Send a few photos and we build you, your team or your family into the film, in wardrobe and in character. Or keep it faceless and let the story carry it. Both work.

What if I don't know what my story is?

That's what the call is for. Almost everyone has it. They've just never said it out loud to someone whose job is to listen for it.

Is it really made with AI?

The world is generated, yes. The story, the script, the casting, the direction, the edit and the taste are human. Think of it as a film crew the size of one laptop, with a director who still has to earn every frame.

Will it look like everyone else's AI video?

No. The difference is direction. Every scene is written before it's built, every frame is checked, and anything that drifts gets re-shot. Watch The Carry above and judge for yourself.

Can you use my real product and logo?

Yes, and we insist on it. Your product goes in with its actual artwork, your logo stays your logo, and every word on screen is typeset by hand so it reads exactly as you wrote it.

How long does it take?

A :30 is usually back within days. A longer short runs a week or two depending on how many scenes and how many rounds of notes. You'll get a date on the call.

What does it cost?

It depends on length and the number of scenes, and it is a fraction of a shoot. Tell us the story on the call and you'll leave with a number.

Can you run it as an ad too?

Yes. The film is the piece most businesses are missing. If you want it in front of people with budget behind it, that's what the rest of Social Fire does.
