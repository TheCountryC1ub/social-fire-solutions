Chapter III — The Writer

The man at the desk.

The desk, just after sundown

Josue Sanchez is an American writer working in short fiction, essays, poems, and pieces made to order. He writes the old way — drafts by hand, revisions read out loud, nothing leaving the desk before it's true.

He believes most people carry at least one story they can't quite get onto paper: the toast they want to give, the letter they've rewritten in their head for years, the family story that deserves better than being half-remembered. His work is getting those stories out of the dark — his own, and yours if you'll trust him with it.

"Good writing doesn't show off. It shows up — and it stays."
