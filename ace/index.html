The story

Two Brothers,
One Bet on Each Other

Ace Energy isn't a founder-and-cofounder arrangement stitched together by a pitch deck. It's Trevor Soderholm, a Northern California firefighter, and his brother Tommy, a Southern California entrepreneur, who looked at each other and asked one simple question:

What could we make if we made it side by side?

The point was never just to launch a drink. It was the doing of it — the early mornings, the phone calls, the wins and setbacks, and the shared belief that two people pushing in the same direction reach places neither could alone.

Golf understands this instinctively. You play your own ball — but the best rounds of your life happen with people pulling for you. Ace Energy was born in that spirit.
