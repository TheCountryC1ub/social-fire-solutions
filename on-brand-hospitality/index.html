The Group

Every layer of the room, considered.

Walk into a great restaurant and everything simply feels right — the menu, the music, the lighting, the pour, the person who greets you at the door. None of that is an accident. It's a hundred deliberate decisions, layered until they disappear.

That's what On Brand Hospitality does. Founded by Adrian Verdin and Torry Cray — passionate restaurateurs who have owned and operated successful restaurants and event spaces in Dallas — the group shapes hospitality businesses at every layer: the food, the beverage, the brand, the team, and the operation that holds it all together.

Every engagement is customized to the room in front of us. A brand-new concept finding its feet and an established operation reaching for the next level need very different things — and get them.

There's a difference between a reservation and an experience. We build the second kind.

The Proof · Our Flagship Concept

Café OLIVIA

When we tell a client how a great room should run, we're not quoting a textbook — we're quoting our own dining room. Café OLIVIA is a chef-driven café and coffee shop with two Dallas locations, and every principle we preach, practiced daily.

The Standard

Clean food, without compromise.

Everything at Café OLIVIA is seed-oil-free — cooked exclusively in olive oil, avocado oil, and butter. Never canola, never soybean. Ingredients lean organic and GMO-free, sourced from trusted Texas purveyors, and made fresh every day.

Breakfast and lunch through the week, brunch on the weekends, plus catering and private events — proof that clean and crave-worthy were never opposites.
