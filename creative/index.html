Questions

Before the call

How many ads can I order?

Any number. One hero video, a ten-ad launch set, or a rolling hundred a month. The call is where we settle the count and the price.

Do you need our photos?

Send your logo, product files and any brand guide you have. If you don't have product photography, we can build the scenes around your real artwork, like the PLAYR1 cans and trucks above.

How fast?

First concepts usually land within days. A full set depends on the count and how many video pieces are in it. You'll get a date on the call.

Can you run the ads too?

Yes. Creative is the piece most businesses are missing, but if you want the campaign managed as well, that's what the rest of Social Fire does.

Will the text in the images be right?

Yes. We never let AI generate words. Every headline, price and URL is typeset by hand, so it reads exactly the way you wrote it.

What if I don't like something?

Tell us. Revisions are part of the order, and because we work fast, a change is usually back the same day.
